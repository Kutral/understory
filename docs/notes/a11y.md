## 2026-08-24 — FocusRestore + remap wiring (feat/a11y-wire)

What was wired:

- `src/ui/shell.ts`: `UnderstoryUi` now owns a `FocusRestore` (from
  `src/ui/remap.ts`). `openPause()` captures `document.activeElement`
  (guarded to `HTMLElement`) before the panel opens; `closePause()` releases
  it after `unpause()` and calls `.focus()` when the element is still
  connected — wrapped in try/catch so a mid-call detach can never throw.
- `src/ui/pause.ts`: `rebind()` no longer hand-rolls collision handling; it
  delegates to `applyRemap()` from remap.ts, so collisions swap (no action is
  ever left keyless) and Escape-as-keybinding is rejected with its
  explanation instead of silently dropped.
- Remap feedback: a small `role="status"` live region
  (`.us-pause__remap-status`, styled in pause.css) sits under the controls
  list and surfaces applyRemap's messages — e.g. "Swapped with Brake." It is
  cleared when a new capture starts and hidden when there is nothing to say.
- Escape during key capture now cancels explicitly: old binding kept, button
  label restored, status shows "Cancelled — <Action> keeps <key>."
